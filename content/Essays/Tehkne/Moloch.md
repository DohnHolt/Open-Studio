Moloch is what Ted Kazcyinski was on about. 

Later, Moloch [was popularised](https://slatestarcodex.com/2014/07/30/meditations-on-moloch/) by essayist Scott Alexander, who for the first time linked the metaphor to game theory: the branch of mathematics that analyses competitive dynamics. For Alexander, Moloch is the perfect metaphor for the incentive structures within our economy that inexorably lead to winner-takes-all outcomes, regardless of the intentions of individual actors. As former poker pro and YouTuber Liv Boeree told me in [a recent film](https://youtu.be/p-r54G30kbo) we put out,

“If there’s a force that’s driving us toward greater complexity, there seems to be an opposing force, a force of destruction that uses competition for ill. The way I see it, Moloch is the god of unhealthy competition, of negative sum games.”
