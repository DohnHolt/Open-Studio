

There are some legitamate concerns around AI might cheer some to hear that Elon Musk signed an open letter asking that we pause AI until we understand it further, but we must ask who is the we in that sentence? Is he really asking for us to collectively reflect on tehkne, contemplate with foresight and artful aesthetic sensibility the future direction we as a culture want to travel? If so, is he asking for the current funding model of Silicon Valley to simultaneously be uprooted? Is he saying the times are urgent, let us slow down and work out a way to integrate a new economic model of economic democracy to allow our reflections on tehkne and political ideas to find the path of least resistance to influence our economic decision making? Or does he mean he isn't fully sure what he thinks right now, but in six months he will be on point with how to proceed with AI and we can finally proceed with the vision he has for society? 

  

We must never forget that apparently the high priests of Silicon Valley themselves do not allow their children access to the tools that they are creating. This is pure corruption at the centre of the Temple and the reason that this goes on is that we are all Thatcher's children now. We are all so separated from each other that we choose to dwell in our caves rather than confront this chaos. We need a messiah to come along and throw them out of the Temple, to say to these contemporary money lenders that an economy built on our attention funded by venture capitalism is not ok. 

  

And I believe that the children can be the messiah. 

  

We need to restory our culture. We need to believe in a future again. We need people who know that knowledge is something that you can play with and reflectively and insightfully create solutions to the world out of. We need people who believe in the boundaries of their freedom because they believe in the boundaries of other people’s freedom. We need people who don’t see human relations as a succession of zero-sum games. We need people who can navigate conflict with humility. We need people who can integrate their ideas for changing the world with their experience of having a capacity to do so whenever they like. We need people brought up on the freedom of autonomy balanced with the responsibility of accepting the freedom of others. We need people who have sat through the endless conversations that that entails and have learnt to contemplate ways of moving forward together, who have learnt to ask the question: what do you need? And know that asking it is as important as asking yourself what do I need? We need a desperate return to tehkne and the way to get back to it is through self-directed education.

  

Our answer to the problems of modernity are not ones that we can rush. We must learn to slow down, we need foresight, yes, but we need contemplation, conversation and coordination, that is how we turn crowdsource the tehkne from within the minds of a group of individual into one of cultural change. 

  
  

Is self-directed education both the tool playing and conflict exploring dimension. Tools are tehkne and conflict and meeting culture is arete?
