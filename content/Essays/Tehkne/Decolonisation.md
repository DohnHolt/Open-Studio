What we arguing for is a decolonisation of childhood, a decolonisation of the mind, and a decolonisation of culture. We are arguing for a freeing of children from the bounds of adults; a freeing of the mind from the reductionist, cartesian, monochronic, materialist, physicalist worldview that has done so much to create this technological society, but from which the path onwards seems destined to end in natural disaster; and a freeing of the mind from a culture that is rooted in competitiveness, conflict, growth and multi-polar, zero-sum games. And we need to undertake this education at all levels and ages of society and as quickly as possible. In Homo Ludens Johan Huizinga states that the play-element is at the heart of culture, this play-element, for animals that are inherently technical, to be a technologist is to be human, is the embodiment of tehkne. We need to deschool ourselves, we need to work together to cultivate tehkne and we need to embrace who we are as creatures with foresight and have the foresight to recognise that we need to slow down to do so. It is not time to build, nor is it time to govern, it is time to listen, it is time to play. It is time to be ethical. It is time again for us to create gods that can come down and give us tehkne.
