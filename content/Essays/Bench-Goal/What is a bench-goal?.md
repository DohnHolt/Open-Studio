On the face of it, it seems a reasonable request. However, I often remind my daughter that words are powerful and it is important not to use them to deliberately wield power, especially by omitting important truths. 

*He fell over* is the second half of the sentence and much more pleasant for her to say and for me to hear than, *I pushed him **and** he fell over*.
