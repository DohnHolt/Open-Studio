Welcome. This site hold two main functions. It holds the essays that I have written on self-directed education and hosts my digital garden. 

I write mainly about young people. Topics you might find here are play, youth rights, self-directed education, consent-based education, self-determination theory, autonomy, agency, relatedness, learning communities, designing experiential education experiences. 

You might also find tangential thoughts on topics such as football, leadership, and philosophy. Some of these intersect with my work with young people. 

I invite you to wander through my thoughts. In case you have never interacted with a digital garden before the thoughts in here are collated rhizomatically and not structured in any way. I write most of my notes in obsidian and have a private vault, but push the notes to here that I believe have warranted some kind of status of finished. 

However, as you will see each note has a tag #notereview. In my obsidian vault that is a tag to trigger the notes to come back to me randomly through a spaced repetition software. A finished note is only finished in the sense that it felt like I had written everything I could at that moment. Reviewing and fleshing out notes is part of the process of keeping evergreen notes. Each note here is just a snapshot of my thoughts the moment that I hit send, by the time you have read them they might be slightly out of sync with how I feel currently. I will likely come back to it and edit it as I refine my thinking. 

In the spirit of working with the garage door up I invite you to comment as much as you want across this site. I wish my thoughts to be challenged, your collective reckoning is the quickest path to me refining my thinking.
