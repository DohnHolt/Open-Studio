One of the main advantages of computers is that they can give you instant feedback on the math problem you are working on. They can tell you if an answer is wrong or right. Or, when working with manipulatives they can give you direct feedback as to what the manipulatives are doing as you are moving them around. This means that you can often move through vastly more iterations of a problem solving approach than in the real world. 

One of the other ways that you can move instantaneously to get feedback is by having [[Instant deletion using geogebra]] so that you can wipe your attempt and start again. 

However, instantaneous feedback sometimes is a drawback when the manipulatives that you are working with become too responsive and people rely on mindless trial and error rather than more thoughtful problem solving.
