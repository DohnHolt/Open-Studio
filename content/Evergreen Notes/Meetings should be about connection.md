Three years ago The Garden had 45 minute meetings that dragged on almost every morning. The system that had been implemented at the point of realisation of "These meetings are way too long" was having a hand signal to reduce "distractions". However, beside the point that that probably extended meetings as when there was a distraction there had to be a pause to address the distraction, and that actually it is quite a punitive system and that [[Naming distractions is ableist]], the real question is does it create greater connection among young people or less, does it create greater connection among young people and adults or less? And furthermore, is that the only solution?

If connection is the goal and distractions are happening it tells you that the length of period in which young people have the capacity to connect has reached it's limit. So the question then becomes this: at fifteen minutes we seemingly reach the capacity for connection, how do we manage to make sure that everything we need to do fits inside that fifteen minutes. Or how do we change the meeting structure to facilitate more connection?

The [[First changes to The Garden meetings]] were made after six months based on an observation that constantly long 45 minute meetings were not efficient but also not in line with our values. A belief in increasing agency in young people to be able to have space for their opinion needs to be balanced with the agency in other young people to say I have listened enough and [[Meetings that decrease agency decrease connection]].

When Oscar left The Garden it became apparent to me in the final month or so that we had missed a trick for the previous three or four months. To facilitate a smooth end to the academic year it was named that maybe we should hang out in the Dragon's nest with the tweens and teens during the day a little more as they might need adult facilitation as they connect with each other. I observed that we had them captured for at least three of the fifteen hours that they were with us every morning and afternoon in meetings. So why do we not use those meetings to build connection, instead of forcing them through intentions and reflections in a very procedural way. The culture was that the act of intentions were more important than the way they were done, and as adults we will revert you to that when you seem to have moved away from the process we as adults have decided. But [[Meetings that decrease agency decrease connection]] and the agency that these young people wanted to express was connection, in their very specific tween way: why do we need roll with that? [[Meetings that are not considered can erode meeting culture]] and the meeting culture was one of low level conflict between mentors and young people as the needs of both were at cross-purposes with each other. Could we not remodel our expectations to meet the young people where they were: [[meeting people's plans where they are for planning]].
