The anthropological literature is clear, [[didactic teaching is rare]], and most children learn about their social, cultural and ecological environment through observation and imitation. This starts with play and may move through to helping in small ways with the [[Chore curriculum]], before children become more and more competent and reach adult levels of competency. 

Whilst learning through observation children are following an active learning process in three distinct ways: children are intrinsically motivated, [[self-directing their learning]], whilst in a state of [[open attention]].
