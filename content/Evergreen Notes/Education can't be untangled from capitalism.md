[[Education as factory model]] was how most of the Victorians who modeled the new education system on the Prussian system saw their work; the emulation of the factory with it's tight schedule of time and knowledge and control.

When people talk of the requirements of education in the modern day they talk about what [[The future skills of 2030]] are and use these claims as the basis for what children will therefore need to learn. It is interesting to note that these skills are often also the same skills that self-directed educators will agree are likely to be required in the future, but the means to get to these same ends vastly differ. That education should be aligned with the needs of tomorrow's global workforce is something worthy of critique, that the means of education force young people towards the ends of capital is worth questioning, but do different means, means of self-direction, that are tending towards the same ends not invalidate the criticism. If the ends are the same then are not the future skills teleological? 

Tolstoy said that learning in childhood should help to instigate freedom and removing compulsion from education allows culture, the free association of people's, to prosper. Education is culture under restraint, but self-directed education, Tolstoy would say, is truly cultural transmission. That the culture is capitalist, that industry us likely to be requiring those skills in 2030, is not going to change, but the means in which young people can approach that future society can be one based on freedom or one based on compulsion. It is only in freedom can culture reside, and it is only in being raised in freedom that young people will see that they can change culture, mould it to suit their needs and not be moulded first by schools and then by society.

This is why unschooling and [[Self Directed Education is a Political Act]], and it is in recognising the political nature of freedom and acting on it that [[Learning communities exist as sites of prefigurative politics]] and through this praxis we can say that [[Learning communities exist as anti-capitalist sites]].
