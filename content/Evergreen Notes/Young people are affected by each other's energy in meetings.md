The energy of other being down brings those who are interested in the meeting down and they can no longer participate to the level of their original engagement. 

When Theo and I had a chat about the culture of community meetings we did so with Hetty and Tobias there. They chose not to participate in our chat and sat on their phones on the other sofa for twenty minutes and this was not distracting. However, when Ben and Paladin entered the yurt and suddenly there were more bodies in the space the energy shifted. Paladin sat next to Theo and was totally disinterested in the topic and watched Rowan playing DS. He was not interrupting at all and was reasonably quiet, but just the energy of a body who didn't care being in the space so close to Theo shifted the whole energy of the conversation. 

Often young people appear tired and sleepy in meetings and then when it is over just want to leave; others have that energy dip carry on over into the next hour of their day. 

However, as good permaculturalists we can utilise this fact as the problem becomes the solution: meetings that are high energy can facilitate greater energy in other young people. One example is [[setting intentions and reflections whilst playing games]].
