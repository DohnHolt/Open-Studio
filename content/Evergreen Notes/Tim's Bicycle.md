Process is the big wheel that is really driving the project whilst product is the small wheel, which is easy to forget is also moving as the big wheel moves. Penny farthings are prone to topple over, and projects are prone to topple over too, if there is not an understanding that the two wheels need to talk to each other because [[Process and product are connected]]. When we can see that the small wheel is connected to the big and that the actions of the big move the small we can see that [[Products are slow moving verbs]] and we need to bear in mind how they move. The best way to pay attention to this relationship is through [[intention and reflection cycles]].
