Using community meetings to gain information from the whole community can often feel like "a waste of time for young people". The question adults should always be asking is "is this the best way to gain the information that I think I need?" Remembering that we need to be clear on [[What is a Thursday meeting]] and what is it for and how do we protect [[Change up meeting culture that honours young people's needs]]. Sometimes we can do that by choosing not to have a meeting if better ways of operating exist, because [[Meetings that are not considered can erode meeting culture]]

Maybe individual chats might take more time but actually get better information as it is much more likely that the conversation will have greater [[Psychological safety]]. 

Maybe a questionnaire might be a better option, or a blend of the two as sometimes different young people would welcome [[Parallel systems outside of meetings to facilitate capturing stated values]]. 

Maybe the idea of a group conversation is important but perhaps doing those in our intention circles might be a better way. Smaller groups, especially ones in which the young people are talking just with their peers will increase [[Psychological safety]] and the smaller amounts of people and the safer space will increase connection and agency. [[Meetings should be about connection]] after all and [[Meetings that decrease agency decrease connection]], whilst increasing agency will increase connection.
