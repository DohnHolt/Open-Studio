Mentors can often struggle to speak witnessing truths to situations due to the vulnerability that an adults much more complex observation and language skills may cause in a young person who is being called out. However, the danger is that by staying in the moment as a passive observer you can risk becoming a [shaming witness](https://www.davidbedrick.com/) to those other young people who are being impacted by the behaviour. 

The question is how do we see past a [[binary distinction of power over and power with]] and settle into a comfortable relationship with our power in relation to young people as being much more situationally appropriate.
