Young people observe that note taking delays the meeting as it slows it down, especially when people have to repeat things for the minute taker. 

Also, the allegation from young people is who reads the notes? What purpose do they even serve? Young people certainly do not. Adults rarely do either; and often when they do they do so on the day and could probably get the information from another adult.
