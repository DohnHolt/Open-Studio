Play is central to children learning and in societies that occupy more complex niches children play for longer and adolescence is extended to incorporate this need for a longer period of learning before adult competency. 

'Foraging and industrial societies have extended adolescence and play is more important for learning than in agrarian societies'[^1]

---
[^1]: The Anthropology of learning in childhood
