The chore curriculum is the work required to be done to maintain the household. In nonindustrialised socieites where the mode of economic production is centred around the household and society operates with an [[open book approach to learning]] children will be constantly surrounded by working adults, this ongoing presence and integration in adult activities means that inevitably they will pick up this curriculum by [[Learning through observation]]. 

This is in essence means that young people are engaged in ongoing  [[Apprenticeships]] in not only the tasks, but the values of the culture.
