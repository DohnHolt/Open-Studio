One of the constraints in [[Creating a learning culture at the Garden]] at The Garden is that there is a belief in the need for young people to have *"agency."* However, if it is true that [“traditional education kills agency by removing choice and interest and progressive education kills agency by removing excellence and competence"](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/zWYtAUkGpTQKCJ6GgyQkAth) what does this mean for us as we try to create a learning culture among young people who are deschooling. What does excellence look like for a young person outside the constraints of school where choice and interest are returned to them but excellence is not necessarily a destination that is actively pursued as an end in itself by adults? 

Is it true that [[The pizza oven project was a well executed project]]? Is it disingenuous to laud it as such, and not be critical of the ways in which it lacks excellence? Does the young person know where it fails to meet excellence? Do they deserve the right to have that explored with them? Is [[Tim's bicycle]] not driving at this notion that process over product is all well and good for a young child in the early years but for an adult product is somewhat important and there is a curve where one falls away in preference to the other; even in [[Indigineous pedagogies]] the play of fashioning arrows as an eight year old has to give way to the product of a successful hunt at some point. 

Whilst Artemis might say we are low demand and claim that that therefore increases autonomy as Bess noted at the time and keeps returning to there are some young people with us for who there is a high level of demand in the space to be highly agentic as the increased  cognitive demands for making plans into realities that exists at The Garden, or the scaffolding or support required for a accessing resources, and by laying all of those demands on young people it does not create an enabling environment as "agency is rate limited by competence". [[High autonomy does not necessarily lead to high agency]] 

By not creating an environment in which excellence as an end goal is celebrated then we fail those with cognitive capacities to drive their own projects and by not supporting others with resourcing support, project preparation and other things that make completion a possibility we are depriving them of the opportunity for completion and without completion there is no chance of excellence, which means a limited future capacity for agency.
