Meetings that feel laborious can put off the young people who want to do the meeting. Change Up meetings that force everyone to participate when only a few care about the chosen awareness can actually put off the people who want to do the purpose of the meeting. Sometimes this is because of the very thought of having to do the meeting but sometimes it is because [[Young people are affected by each other's energy in meetings]].
